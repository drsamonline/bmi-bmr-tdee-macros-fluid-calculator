What these mean
BMI
Weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²). Categorised with Indian/Asia-Pacific or WHO cut-offs.
Body fat %
Estimated from BMI, age and sex (Deurenberg). A population estimate, not a measurement.
Lean mass
Fat-free mass = weight × (1 − body-fat %).
Waist / height & waist / hip
Central-obesity risk ratios. Waist-to-height ≥0.5; waist-to-hip >0.90 (men) or >0.85 (women) is elevated.
BSA
Body surface area (Mosteller), used for drug dosing.
CrCl
Creatinine clearance (Cockcroft–Gault) from age, weight and serum creatinine. A population estimate; needs steady-state renal function.
Ideal / adjusted weight
Devine ideal body weight, and IBW + 0.25 × (actual − IBW).
BMR / TDEE
Resting energy (Mifflin-St Jeor) and total daily energy with the activity multiplier.
Protein / fat / carbs / fiber
Daily macro targets; carbohydrate fills the remaining calories.
Fluid
30 mL/kg/day baseline, adjusted for climate, exercise, fever and breastfeeding.
Protein scales 0.8-2.0 g/kg by activity (DRI/ACSM). Fluid uses a 30 mL/kg baseline adjusted for climate, exercise and clinical flags. Always apply clinical judgement for renal, cardiac and paediatric patients.

    References & formulas
    • BMR: Mifflin MD et al. Am J Clin Nutr 1990;51:241-7 (Mifflin-St Jeor).
    • TDEE: activity multipliers, standard Harris-Benedict/Mifflin tiers.
    • BMI cut-offs: WHO international and WHO Asia-Pacific / ICMR Indian cut-offs.
    • Protein & fiber: IOM Dietary Reference Intakes; ACSM/NSCA protein range 0.8-2.0 g/kg.
    • Fluid: 30 mL/kg/day adult baseline (EFSA/IOM general guidance).
    • Ideal body weight: Devine BJ, Drug Intell Clin Pharm 1974. Adjusted BW = IBW + 0.25 x (actual - IBW).
    • BSA: Mosteller RD. N Engl J Med 1987;317:1098.
    • Body fat: Deurenberg P et al. Br J Nutr 1991;65:105-14.
    • Waist: IDF/WHO Asia-Pacific cut-offs (men >90 cm, women >80 cm).
